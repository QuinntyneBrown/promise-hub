# Attendance

Roll Call: today's sessions and their rosters, check-in (now, earlier or late), absences (excused or not), walk-ins, sign-out, no-show alerts and ending a session (L2-017, L2-018, L2-028, L2-095), all through `AttendanceWriter` and scoped by `SessionAccess` (assigned sessions, and a mentor's cohorts, ADR 0065); a cohort's history and rates (`AttendanceHistoryHandler`) and audited corrections (`CorrectAttendanceHandler`, L2-097); Quick Cards and approved pickups.
