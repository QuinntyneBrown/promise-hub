Progression framework

The stages youth grow through, and the competencies that earn each one.

1. Explorer

Finding their place in the group

  • Serves others
    Notices what needs doing and helps without being asked.
  • Works with others
    Listens, takes turns and helps the group decide.

2. Contributor

Takes part and helps others take part

  • Speaks up
    Shares ideas in the group and says when something is wrong.
  • Follows through
    Finishes what they start and keeps their promises.

3. Leader

Leads others in the cohort

  • Leads a small group
    Guides a discussion and keeps a small group on track.

How youth advance

A youth reaches a stage once their mentor marks every competency in it, and in the stages before it, Proficient or Mastered.

  1. Explorer
  2. Contributor
  3. Leader

Mentors assess these competencies on each youth’s record. Badges can count toward one, such as Servant Heart toward Serves others.
