Last in Line

Leading by Serving · Lesson 2 of 3 · 15 min

Read

Mark 10:35–45

“For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many.”

Mark 10:45
James and John wanted the best seats, one on each side of Jesus. It’s an honest ask: most of us want to be seen, to matter, to lead. In the world, greatness flows downhill: the more important you are, the more people serve you. Here it flips. “Whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant.” This week, watch for one person you can quietly serve without being asked.

Reflect before you move on

Where in your week are you being asked to serve when you’d rather be served?
