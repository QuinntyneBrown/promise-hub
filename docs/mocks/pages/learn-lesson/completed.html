Pick Up the Towel

Leading by Serving · Lesson 1 of 3 · 12 min

The lesson

Reading: The captain and the kit bags

The night before the big game, the team captain stayed behind to pack the kit bags while everyone else went home. Nobody asked her to. Nobody saw it, either, until the coach found the bags lined up at the door the next morning. Leading isn’t a title. It’s noticing what needs doing and doing it, especially when nobody is watching.

Think it over

Who picked up the towel for you this week? Hold that thought for your reflection.
