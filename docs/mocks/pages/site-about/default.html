About Harbourview Youth Collective

Harbourview Youth Collective runs free after-school tutoring, Saturday sports and one-to-one mentoring for children and youth in Hamilton’s north end. Families sign up once and hear from us in their own language.

Our promise

Safe, known and cared for

  • Screened adults
    Every adult who works with children is screened and reference checked.
  • Guardian consent
    We ask a guardian’s consent before a child takes part.
  • Health on hand
    Allergies and medications are on hand at every session.
