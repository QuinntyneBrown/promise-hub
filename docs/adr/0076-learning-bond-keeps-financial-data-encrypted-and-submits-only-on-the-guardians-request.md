# 0076: Learning Bond keeps financial data encrypted and submits only on the guardian's request

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-29

## Context

`L1-026` to `L1-029` (`L2-135` to `L2-163`) add the twelfth module, **Learning Bond**: an organization turns it on behind a versioned data-handling agreement, guardians claim the Canada Learning Bond and check child benefits from a text link with no account, and staff see completion status only. The module handles social insurance numbers (SINs), tax-filing status, household size, marital status and uploaded documents, which no other module holds, and it talks to systems outside PromiseHub: RESP providers, benefit programs and a language model. The mocks are in `docs/mocks/` (`settings-learning-bond`, `learning-bond-*`, `guardian-learning-bond`, `export-learning-bond`, `withdraw-learning-bond-consent`).

PromiseHub already has what the module builds on: `ModuleCatalog` and the module gates (ADR 0006, `L2-004`, `L2-005`), `GuardianAccess` and trusted devices for guardian links (`L2-026`), `GuardianConsent` with typed signatures (ADR 0046, `L2-055`), `MessageWriter` and the per-tenant send job with the SMS cap and STOP (`L2-021`, `L2-022`, `L2-034`), `IAuditWriter` (`L2-032`), `NotificationWriter` (`L2-040`), `ReportExport` with 7-day links (`L2-025`), `IFileStore` and `IPaymentProvider` as the model of a vendor behind an abstraction (ADR 0075).

## Decision

- **One feature everywhere, named `learning-bond`.** Backend `PromiseHub.Domain/LearningBond/` and `PromiseHub.Application/LearningBond/`, endpoints in `PromiseHub.Api/Endpoints/LearningBondEndpoints.cs` and `LearningBondGuardianEndpoints.cs`, the frontend library `frontend/projects/learning-bond/` behind the `LEARNING_BOND_API` token in `frontend/projects/api/`, designs in `docs/detailed-designs/learning-bond/`, tests in `e2e/tests/learning-bond/`. The library maps to one module, `ModuleId.LearningBond`, and loads only while it is on; the guardian pages are routes of the same library under `/g/learning-bond/:token`.
- **A module that needs two.** `ModuleCatalog.Needs(ModuleId.LearningBond)` is Roster and Messages. `ModuleCatalog.Suite` is unchanged, so no preset turns it on. Turning it on also requires an acceptance of the current agreement version; `SaveModulesHandler` asks `LearningBondAgreementGate` and answers 422 "Accept the Learning Bond data-handling agreement first." Staff endpoints are grouped under `/learning-bond` with `RequireModule(ModuleId.LearningBond)`, except the agreement routes (`/learning-bond/agreement/**`), which require `ManageSettings` and Roster and Messages on, since the agreement is read and accepted before the module can be on; the signer's anonymous link is `/sign/learning-bond/{token}`, resolved by `SignerLinkAccess`; guardian endpoints under `/g/learning-bond/{token}` resolve the organization through `GuardianAccess` and then check the module, so an organization with the module off answers 404 everywhere, including webhooks, whose organization comes from the stored application.
- **Platform-owned catalogues, versioned.** The agreement text (`LearningBondAgreement`: version, effective date, three sections), the RESP provider list (`RespProvider`: name, provinces, channel, processing window), the estimate rule (`LearningBondRule`: amounts, effective date) and the benefits catalogue (`BenefitCatalogueEntry`: name, level, province or municipality, eligibility rule, estimate formula, source link, renewal rule, channel) are platform data with no organization id, seeded by the Cli and never editable by an organization. Every acceptance, estimate and benefit check records the version it used.
- **Settings and launch per organization.** `LearningBondSettings` (one row per organization): `Coverage` (`AllPrograms` or `ChosenPrograms` with program ids), `StatusRoles` (Admin and Program director always present), `SenderName`, and the launch (`LaunchMode` `Scheduled` or `AtTheDoor`, `LaunchDate`, `CancelledAt`). `LearningBondAgreementAcceptance` and `LearningBondSignerRequest` (single-use hashed token, 14 days, one open per organization) record who accepted which version.
- **A claim per child, a household's intake once.** `LearningBondInvitation` (household, sent at, channel) stops a second invitation within 7 days. `LearningBondConsent` is its own entity with `Scope` `LearningBond` or `Benefits`, child, guardian, typed name, wording version, language and time; it never reuses `GuardianConsent`, so no other consent counts as Learning Bond consent and vice versa. `LearningBondIntake` (one per household: guardian SIN, tax-filing answer, benefits facts) and `LearningBondClaim` (one per consented child: child SIN, status, paused reason, reminder choices, estimate and rule version). `LearningBondApplication` (kind `LearningBond` or `Benefit`, provider or program, fields as sent, `ReviewVersion`, status `Draft`, `ReadyToSubmit`, `Submitted`, `Received`, `Deposited`, `Cancelled`, amounts, dates) is the record a submission, a webhook and the dashboard all read. Household status (`Not invited` … `Not interested`) is computed from these records by `LearningBondStatus`, never stored twice.
- **Encrypted at the field, destroyed on schedule.** `EncryptedSin` is a value object of `Ciphertext` and `Last3`; `ILearningBondCipher` (Infrastructure `LearningBond/`) encrypts and decrypts under a key read from configuration or a key vault, never stored in the database, and answers `Unavailable` when the key is missing, which the handlers turn into 503 "Learning Bond is unavailable right now. Nothing was lost." Documents go to `IFileStore` encrypted the same way. The only decrypting call site is `SubmitApplicationHandler`, which builds the provider payload in memory. `LearningBondDestructionJob` (daily, per tenant) deletes ciphertext 24 hours after every application that used it is `Received` or `Cancelled`, deletes sent documents after 24 hours, and closes claims idle for 180 days as `Not completed`. DTOs for staff carry status and dates only; DTOs for guardians carry `Last3` only; `IAuditWriter` entries name fields, never values.
- **Submission is a guardian request with a version.** `POST /g/learning-bond/{token}/applications/{id}/submit` requires the application's current `ReviewVersion` (409 otherwise), a trusted device, and the module on. `SubmitApplicationHandler` marks the application `Submitted` and records an idempotency key before calling `IRespProviderChannel.SubmitAsync` or `IBenefitProgramChannel.SubmitAsync`, so a repeat answers the same 202 and sends nothing; a refused or unreachable channel resets `ReadyToSubmit`. Infrastructure registers `FakeRespProviderChannel` and `FakeBenefitProgramChannel` until a provider has credentials, as `UnconfiguredPaymentProvider` does for payments (ADR 0075). Webhooks (`POST /learning-bond/webhooks/providers/{provider}`, `/programs/{program}`) verify the vendor's signature through `IWebhookSignatureVerifier`, find the application by the vendor's reference, take the organization from it, and apply each event once by its id.
- **The agent prepares; the server decides.** `LearningBondAgent` (Application) talks to `ILanguageModel` (Infrastructure `LearningBond/`) with a fixed system prompt per step, the household language, first names, ages, statuses and computed figures, and nothing else: `AgentRedactor` removes 9-digit numbers and personal fields before anything is stored or sent. Its tools are `GetStatus`, `GetEstimate`, `ListBenefits`, `ExplainStep` and `OfferCallback`, each a server method authorized against the link's household; there is no tool for consent, submit, withdraw or another household. When the model answers nothing within 10 s, `AgentFallbackCopy` supplies the approved wording of the step in the household's language, so the flow completes without the model. Replies are capped at 60 words, stored on `AgentConversation` for the household, and never answered to staff.
- **Messages through Messages.** Invitations, follow-ups and reminders are queued with `MessageWriter` as `MessageSource.LearningBond`, one per household, in the household's language, with the platform text from `LearningBondTexts`; the send job applies the SMS cap, STOP and email fallback. `LearningBondLaunchJob` runs at 10:00 on the launch date in the organization's time zone; `LearningBondReminderJob` sends due reminders (`LearningBondReminder`: kind, date, turned off) once a day. Both run per tenant and send nothing while the module is off or the organization is suspended.
- **Figures computed on read, suppressed below five.** `LearningBondDashboardHandler` and `ExportLearningBondHandler` aggregate `LearningBondApplication` rows by covered program and period, split Learning Bond and benefits, and replace any figure resting on fewer than 5 households with "Fewer than 5" (or "<5" and blank dollars in the CSV), as `L2-024` does; the export is a `ReportExport` of kind `LearningBond` (`ReportExport` gains a `Kind`).
- **Deferred.** Real provider and program channels and their signatures; the agreement's legal text; the first municipal catalogue entries beyond Hamilton; a funder view across organizations (crosses the tenant boundary, `L1-001`); the youth-claims-for-themselves path for 18 to 20 year olds (the page only says they can).

## Consequences

- `ModuleId` gains `LearningBond`; `ModuleCatalog.Name` and `Needs` know it; `settings-modules` and `onboard-modules` show it last with "Needs Roster and Messages".
- Migrations add the platform tables (`LearningBondAgreements`, `RespProviders`, `LearningBondRules`, `BenefitCatalogueEntries`) and the tenant tables (`LearningBondSettings`, `LearningBondAgreementAcceptances`, `LearningBondSignerRequests`, `LearningBondInvitations`, `LearningBondConsents`, `LearningBondIntakes`, `LearningBondClaims`, `LearningBondApplications`, `LearningBondDocuments`, `LearningBondReminders`, `LearningBondCallRequests`, `AgentConversations`, `AgentMessages`), every tenant table with the organization id and the tenant filter.
- `MessageSource` gains `LearningBond`; `Permission` is unchanged (`ManageSettings` for settings and the agreement, `ViewReports` for the dashboard and export, `StatusRoles` from settings for the household list).
- The seed gives Harbourview the module on with an accepted agreement and claims in several statuses, and Northgate the module off.
- The fake API in `e2e/fixtures/` answers every `/learning-bond` and `/g/learning-bond` route from seeded data, including the module-off 404s.
- Every Learning Bond integration test includes a cross-tenant case and an assertion that no staff response carries a SIN, income, marital status, household size, benefit name, estimate or deposit amount.
