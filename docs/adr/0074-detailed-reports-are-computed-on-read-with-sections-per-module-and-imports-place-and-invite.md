# 0074: Detailed reports are computed on read, with a section per module; imports place youth in cohorts and invite them

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-28

## Context

Prompt 80 ports Waypoint's journey 29 (Report, export and import): `L2-REPORT-001` standard reports, `L2-REPORT-003` data export, `L2-REPORT-004` roster import and `L2-ADMIN-004` the progress board, as `L2-127` to `L2-131`. Waypoint is single-tenant, has one reporting service over every table, filters by date range, cohort, track and mentor, exports CSV, PDF and Google Sheets, and imports youth accounts by email with a Missing consent status. PromiseHub already has Reports (`L2-024`, `ReportCalculator`) and its CSV export (`L2-025`, `ReportExport`, 7-day link), roster import (`L2-010`, `ImportSession`), youth invitations from the child record (`L2-051`, ADR 0045), learning progress per youth membership (`LearnerProgress`, ADR 0051), attendance marks (ADR 0065), private mentor check-ins (ADR 0061) and at-risk flags (ADR 0062). A module that is off answers 404 and is never mentioned.

## Decision

- **Computed on read, never stored.** `DetailedReportsHandler` (`Application/Reports/`) computes the cohort report, the youth report, the staff activity report and the progress board from the tables on every request, inside the tenant filter. Nothing is denormalised, so a lesson done or a session marked shows on the next read.
- **One endpoint per report under the Reports gate.** `GET /reports/cohorts/{programId}`, `/reports/youth/{childId}`, `/reports/staff` and `/reports/progress/{programId}` need the Reports module and `ViewReports` (admins, program directors, site leads). The staff report also needs Mentoring and the progress board Learning: their endpoints answer 404 while that module is off, and their routes redirect to Today.
- **A section per module, null while off.** In the cohort and youth reports, learning is null while Learning is off, attendance while Roll Call is off, and at-risk counts, check-in counts and mentoring while Mentoring is off. The frontend renders a section only when it is present and lists in Detailed reports only the reports whose modules are on.
- **What counts.** A cohort is a program (ADR 0043). Learning completion is lessons done of the published lessons of the program's track, read through `LearnerProgress` for the child's youth membership; a child with no youth account has no learning (No account). A module is Done when its lessons are done and its required quizzes passed. Attendance uses `AttendanceMarks` (attended of marked). Retention is active of all enrollments. At risk is an open flag. Mentor check-ins are counted with their date and engagement only; their topics, actions and private notes never leave the mentor (ADR 0061). Reviews are submissions a mentor accepted or sent back (their `SubmissionEvent` by the mentor's name). Load is Overloaded above 8 youth and Underloaded below 4 (Waypoint's target of 4 to 8).
- **Exports reuse ReportExport.** `POST /reports/exports` gains `kind` (`Summary` by default, `Cohort`, `Progress`, `Staff`) with `programId` and `days`; each writes the rows its report shows as CSV with names, stored for 7 days and audited with the kind and row count. Small-count masking stays on the summary's aggregates (L2-024); the detailed exports are named rows for staff with `ViewReports`.
- **Imports place and invite.** `ImportField` gains `Cohort` and `YouthEmail` after `Skip`, so stored mappings keep their numbers. A cohort is matched to an active program of the organization by its normalised name; the row's child is enrolled in it instead of the program chosen for everyone. A youth email issues one youth invitation through `InvitationIssuer` after the import is saved, under L2-051's rules (ages 12 to 18, no account or waiting invitation, an email nobody uses). Mapping `YouthEmail` needs Learning and `ManageStaff`; otherwise the mapping answers 422 on that field. Consent is asked when the youth signs in to Learning (L2-056), so Waypoint's Missing consent status is not ported.
- **Deferred.** Date ranges, track and mentor filters on the cohort and youth reports; comparing with another cohort or the previous term; charts over time and weekly engagement; reports scoped to a mentor's own youth; PDF and Google Sheets exports and the audit-log export; live updates of the progress board; Waypoint's Mentor, Track and Status import columns.

## Consequences

- `DetailedReportsHandler` and its DTOs in `Application/Reports/`; `ReportsHandler.ExportAsync` dispatches by kind; `ReportEndpoints` maps the new routes.
- `ColumnMatcher`, `ImportRows`, `SaveMappingHandler` and `RunImportHandler` learn the two fields; `ImportPreviewDto` and `ImportSummaryDto` gain the placement and invitation counts and lists. No migration: mappings are JSON and the new fields are enum values appended at the end.
- The reports library gains `CohortReportPage`, `YouthReportPage`, `StaffReportPage`, `ProgressBoardPage` and `ExportReportDialog`; the app mounts `reports/staff` (Reports and Mentoring) and `reports/progress` (Reports and Learning) through `moduleRoute`; `PhTable` gains a footer row.
- The fake API computes the four reports and the new exports from its seeded learning, attendance and mentoring data and mirrors the import's new fields.
