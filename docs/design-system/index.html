PromiseHub design system

The visual and interaction language for staff, guardians and youth-serving organizations.

Start

This is the design reference extracted from 267 screens and 1602 HTML mocks. Start with foundations, inspect a component’s states and accessibility contract, then use patterns to compose a screen. Copy semantic classes and the DTCG token export into the shared Angular components library as implementation proceeds. The application remains a scaffold.

Foundations

ColourViolet identifies primary actions; semantic status colours always carry written meaning.TypographyRounded system type keeps names, instructions and high-priority information legible.SpacingA 4px base scale sets consistent inside and between spacing.LayoutOne mobile-first grid governs columns, margins, gutters and page widths.ElevationFour shadow levels and explicit layers distinguish surfaces without competing with content.ShapeFive ordinary radii and one full pill radius establish a consistent rounded language.MotionMovement explains change and remains optional.IconographyOutline icons support written labels; colour alone never identifies a medical or attendance state.ThemingChange semantic roles once to update every component and mock.ResponsiveLet content wrap and controls stay reachable from a 360px phone to an office laptop.AccessibilityWCAG 2.2 AA is a component contract and a screen-level responsibility.ContentSpeak calmly and directly to staff and families, in their language.

Components

ButtonTrigger a single explicit action, such as checking a child in or sending a slip. A disabled button points with aria-describedby to the visible text that says why, as a blocked Launch does.Button groupGroup related choices, such as a youth's tracks, so exactly one is pressed without creating several primary actions.LinkNavigate to a household, event or record with a named destination.MenuOffer secondary account actions without obscuring the current task.Text fieldCollect one labelled value with persistent guidance and a specific error.TextareaCollect a message, incident description or permission text over several lines.SelectChoose one known value using the native platform control.CheckboxSelect independent permissions, recipients or consent acknowledgements.Radio groupChoose exactly one option from a labelled set.SwitchChange a named preference while retaining a visible on or off state.Date pickerEnter dates with a native date input and a visible format label.File uploadSelect a roster CSV through a labelled file input; dragging is optional.Form fieldKeep the label, control, optional help, counter and error together.Form layoutArrange related fields and one primary submit action with a linked error summary.Top barOrient staff and keep primary navigation and account actions within reach.Sidebar navigationExpose a complete labelled list of permitted destinations.Bottom navigationKeep frequent staff destinations reachable on phones.TabsSwitch between related views of one record without losing its context.BreadcrumbShow the parent path back to a list or organization.PaginationMove through a bounded set while preserving filters and announcing the result count.StepperShow the current step and completed work during enrollment or setup.Skip linkLet keyboard users move directly past repeated navigation to the main content.CardGroup one related set of facts and actions on a distinct surface.AccordionReveal related details on demand through a native disclosure.DialogAsk for a focused decision or edit while preserving the calling context.DrawerPresent the full navigation as an edge sheet on smaller screens.TooltipClarify an icon control on focus and hover without carrying essential instructions.DividerSeparate alternatives with text or draw a decorative rule.Container, grid and stackCompose consistent pages through shared columns, gutters and vertical rhythm.TableCompare records through captioned columns with reachable row actions.ListScan related records with one clear primary link per item. A row may carry a paragraph of text (.list__text) under its meta, as for a youth's shared reflection, and a cluster of buttons under it for a row without a link, as on an at-risk flag.Description listPresent labelled facts, such as contact details and approved pickups.AvatarIdentify a person by initials or image alongside their written name.BadgeExpress a named status, role or meaningful count without relying on colour alone.ChipRepresent a filter choice, removable input value or short static category.StatHighlight a labelled count without losing the period or denominator.TimelineRead a chronological history with named actors and times.ToastAcknowledge a completed action and offer a short recovery action when available.AlertKeep important page or field feedback visible near the affected task.BannerKeep a persistent page-wide condition visible with a clear recovery action.Inline messageAssociate concise help or a corrective error with its field.Progress barShow meaningful completion with an accessible label and numerical value.SpinnerIndicate an ongoing short operation alongside a specific loading message.SkeletonReserve the shape of expected content while its parent is busy.Empty stateExplain a first run, filtered result or unavailable state and offer one next action.Error pageExplain why a destination is unavailable while keeping a safe way back.Brand markIdentify PromiseHub without replacing the accessible organization or application name.Attendance rosterKeep each child's identity, medical flag and attendance action on one readable row. Late reads as checked in and excused as absent, each said in words in the row's meta, never by colour alone.Medical flagSignal that allergy or medication information is available on the child's Quick Card.Quick CardPut emergency information first with readable contact and pickup details.Call listList authorized contacts in their intended calling order with explicit phone actions.Sync statusDistinguish pending local work from confirmed synchronization in words and colour.Guardian shellKeep guardian pages simple, with organization identity and a labelled language choice.SummaryPresent a short review of submitted or consent-related facts before confirmation.SignatureDisplay the signer's name and recorded metadata; it is not a drawing canvas.Slip eventSummarize the event being consented to before a guardian signs.SMS previewPreview the guardian message with language and length information.Push notificationShow a concise lock-screen-style message with a named organization and destination.Recipient countMake the size of the intended audience visible before sending.Bulk action barShow the number selected and the actions that will affect those records.Roster import mappingMap imported CSV columns to named fields with sample values for review.Plan meterShow billing usage with both a count and a labelled progress track.Attendance historyShow past attendance with a readable status legend and per-session meaning.Notification panelReview persistent notifications with unread state and a clear destination.WizardGuide organization setup through a bounded sequence with Back, Skip and one primary action.Onboarding receiptConfirm the records created during setup in a short concrete list.Question panelGroup one survey question with its response or completion status.Search resultGroup household, child and event matches by meaningful record type.Icon tileProvide a decorative leading icon beside a fully named object.Status dotPair a short status word with a small colour marker for fast scanning.QR codePresent a high-contrast machine-readable plate with a written destination and a link alternative.Authentication cardGroup account access fields and recovery links in a readable, bounded surface.Error summaryList invalid fields with links so users can correct a failed submission without searching.Invitation rowPair a staff email address with the role being granted before an invitation is sent.Scripture blockSet a quoted passage apart from the teaching with its reference, read at the lesson's text size.Video playerPlay a lesson video with native, keyboard-operable controls and a failure state that keeps the lesson readable.Code sampleShow a read-only, scrollable example of code with an accessible name.Code playgroundEdit a small page of code, run it in a sandboxed preview and keep the lesson's checklist nearby.Quiz optionOffer one answer of a quiz question around a native radio or checkbox, and mark it correct, wrong or missed in words in the review.Achievement badgeShow a badge youth earn as a medal with its name and how it is earned; a locked badge says so in words, not only in grey. A collection (.achievement-badges) lists several as a labelled grid that wraps from one column on a phone.Message threadShow a conversation between a mentor and a youth as a log of messages, oldest first: theirs on the start side, the reader's own on the end side, each with who and when in words, day separators, a sending or failed message said in words with Try again, and a labelled composer with Send at the end; read only for a supervisor.Organization logoShow an organization's own uploaded logo in a square frame on a subtle surface, with its written name nearby, or its initials until it has one.Site navFrame the public website at the top: the organization's name linking home, its public pages as links with the current one marked, and Sign in and Apply at the end; below 768px the links wrap onto their own row and nothing hides behind a menu.Site footerClose every public page with the organization's name, the same pages plus Privacy, and a quiet line with the city and who powers the pages.Agent messageLet the Learning Bond agent say one short line per step in a bubble beside its face, with an optional hint under it; the line is the approved fixed wording until the model rephrases it, and a screen reader hears who is speaking.Email previewPreview an email a guardian or a signer receives, with its title, text, one action and a quiet footer naming the sender, inside a card on the page that sends it.Benefit cardShow one child benefit with its name, a badge saying whether to apply, send a document or that the family isn't eligible, its level and estimated amount, a line of why, one action and its source.Step markerNumber the steps of what happens next in a round disc, and show a check once a step is done, with Done also said in words in the row.CalendarShow a month of sessions by week, Monday first: each day lists its sessions as links with their time, today is marked, and a cancelled session says so in words and is struck through. Below 640px it becomes an agenda of only the days with sessions.

Patterns

FormsEdit a household or enroll a child through a labelled, reviewable flow.Feedback and loadingChoose feedback by duration, scope and whether staff can safely continue.Empty and error statesExplain the cause and offer one useful recovery action.Navigation and page structureKeep staff context and guardian reading order consistent across screen sizes.Dialogs and overlaysUse a dialog for a short focused task and a page for long or multi-step work.Data tables and listsChoose a list for scanning and a table for column comparison; preserve filtering and selection.NotificationsUse temporary feedback for acknowledgements and persistent records for work needing attention.Content and toneUse direct, calm language that tells families what happened and what to do next.
