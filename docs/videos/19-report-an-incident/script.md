# 19 · Report an incident

Children trip, get sick and sometimes get hurt. When that happens at a program, the organization owes the family and its insurer a clear record, written while the details are fresh. This video shows how Jaylen, the site lead at tutoring night, reports Mateo's fall from his phone, and who can read it afterwards.

## What incident reports promise

Any staff member can report an incident. The form asks for the facts in a fixed order: the type, the child, when and where, what happened, what was done, whether the guardian was reached, and how serious it was. A report missing any of those is not saved, and the page names each missing fact. A complete report needs review and is emailed at once to the program director. Only supervisors and the reporter can read it, and every view is recorded.

## Jaylen reports Mateo's fall

Here is Jaylen's phone. He chooses Injury and Mateo, the time and the stairwell, writes what happened and what he did, notes that Carmen was reached, and marks it minor. The note at the bottom says the report goes to Marisol. He submits, and the report opens with its status: needs review.

## Who can read it

Back in the log, Jaylen sees only the reports he wrote. A youth worker following a link to someone else's report is told it can't be found. Marisol and Tom, as supervisors, see the whole log with open reports first. Another organization never sees any of it.

## How it is tested

Backend tests cover the form's children and supervisor, a report with missing facts saving nothing, a complete report needing review with the email and the audit entries, reporters seeing only their own reports, supervisors seeing the log, another organization refused, and the module switched off. The browser tests follow Jaylen and Marisol on phone and desktop, with an accessibility check on the form, the error summary and the report.

## Recap

Things to remember. Facts in a fixed order, and nothing saved until they are all there. Reports go straight to the program director. Only supervisors and the reporter can read them, and every view is recorded. Next, reviewing the report.
