How it is tested

  • LateAndExcusedTests and MentorRollCallTests: flags, counts, sign-out, replay, mentor scope, tenancy, Roll Call off
  • AttendanceHistoryTests and CorrectAttendanceTests: statuses, rates, youth, roles, every check, audit
  • late-and-excused, mentor-attendance, offline-attendance and attendance-history specs on phone and laptop, with axe

Things to remember

  • Late is a check-in after the start; excused is an accepted absence, in the one Roll Call
  • Mentors take attendance for their cohorts’ sessions, online or offline
  • History shows sessions and youth with rates; corrections are audited with a reason
