# 31 · Coordinate staff coverage

A session with no adult assigned is a promise nobody is keeping. This video shows how Priya, the volunteer coordinator, sees the next two weeks at a glance, fills a gap, and invites a new volunteer, without needing admin rights.

## What coverage promises

The coverage page lists every session in the next fourteen days with who is working it. A session with no one assigned is marked as a gap. Coordinators, site leads, directors and admins can assign staff, and that assignment is what lets a youth worker run the session. Coordinators can invite new youth workers, but only admins can give out other roles. Every assignment is recorded.

## Priya fills a gap

Here is Staff coverage on Priya's laptop. Tutoring night has Jaylen and Aisha. The field trip on the fourteenth has no one, so it's marked as a gap. She taps Assign staff, ticks Aisha and Jaylen, and saves. The gap is gone, and Aisha will see the trip among her sessions.

## Inviting a volunteer

When Priya invites someone, the only role on offer is youth worker. If she tried another role, the server would refuse it and say to ask an admin. Youth workers who open this page are told who assigns staff.

## How it is tested

Backend tests cover the coverage list with gaps, assigning staff and the youth worker then seeing the session, the audit entry, coordinators limited to inviting youth workers, youth workers refused, another organization's session not found, and people from outside the organization refused. The browser tests follow Priya and Aisha on phone and desktop with accessibility checks.

## Recap

Things to remember. Two weeks of sessions at a glance. Gaps stand out. Coordinators assign and invite volunteers. Next, what each role can see about a child.
