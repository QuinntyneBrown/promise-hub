# 33 · Monitor today's work

A program director starts the day wanting one answer: what needs me? This video shows how Today brings tonight's sessions, the slips still out, what's coming up and the work waiting into one page, built only from the modules the organization has switched on.

## What Today promises

Today reads one summary from the server. It counts the active children and households. It lists slips still collecting signatures, with how many are unsigned. It shows the next two weeks of events, and the work waiting for you: incident reports to review, enrollments to review and pickup requests. Each count is a link to the screen where you do that work. A module that is switched off has no card, no count and no link.

## Marisol opens Today

Here is Today on Marisol's laptop. Tutoring night is three of four checked in. One incident and three enrollments are waiting for her. The field trip slip still has three unsigned. Saturday Sports and the field trip are coming up.

## With only Roster on

Now the same organization with only Roster switched on. The counts and what's coming up are still there. There is no session card, no slips and no incident review, and nothing links to a screen that is off.

## Phones and loading

On a phone the cards stack in one column. While the summary loads, skeletons hold its shape; if it fails, Today says so and offers to try again.

## How it is tested

Backend tests cover the counts, slips due, coming up and tasks for a director, cards of modules that are off left out, no review work for a youth worker, another organization counting only its own records, and Today answering not found with Roster off. The browser tests follow Marisol, Aisha and Grace on phone and desktop with accessibility checks.

## Recap

Things to remember. One summary, composed on the server. Every count links to its work. Nothing of an off module appears. Next, searching records across the organization.
