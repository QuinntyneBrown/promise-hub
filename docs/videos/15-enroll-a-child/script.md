# 15 · Enroll a child

Every season starts with families signing up. This video shows how Marisol opens Winter Camp for enrollment and shares one link, and how a family new to Harbourview enrolls their daughter from that link on a phone, with no account, in a few minutes. It also shows what a returning family sees, and what happens when a program is full or enrollment has closed.

## What enrollment promises

Each program has one enrollment link that never changes, even if enrollment closes and opens again. A guardian checks it's them with a six-digit code sent to the phone or email they type. A returning family finds last season's household and children already filled in, in their own language. New families enter the household, each child, allergies and medication, and agree to the season consent. Nothing joins the roster until staff review it, and when the program is full the child goes on the waitlist with a position.

## Marisol opens enrollment

Here is the Winter Camp program on Marisol's laptop. She taps Open enrollment, confirms, and gets the link to share by text, email or paper. The program page now shows the button to close enrollment and how many children are waiting for review.

## A new family enrolls

Now Ngozi, new to Harbourview, opens the link on her phone. She types her mobile number and the code that arrives. Her number is already filled in on the household step, so she adds her name. On the children step she enters Ada, her date of birth and her shellfish allergy. The review shows what she entered. She agrees to the season consent and submits. The thank-you page says Ada is waiting for review, and that the family will hear back by text or email.

## Returning families and closed links

When Carmen opens the same link, her household and both children are already there, and the page is in Spanish. If Winter Camp were full, Ada would be on the waitlist with her number. And once Marisol closes enrollment, the link says so and gives the organization's number instead.

## How it is tested

Backend tests cover opening and closing with a link that stays the same, the code going to the typed contact, the lock after three wrong codes, a returning family filled in, a new family's household, child and medical details, the season consent, the waitlist position when the program is full, a closed link refusing submissions, site leads refused, another organization's families never matched, and the roster module switched off. The browser tests follow both the director and the family on phone and desktop, with an accessibility check on every step.

## Recap

Things to remember. One link per program that never changes. A code to the contact the guardian types, and returning families filled in. Nothing joins the roster until staff review it. Next, reviewing the enrollments.
