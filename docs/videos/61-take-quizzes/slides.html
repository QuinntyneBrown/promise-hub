Taking the quiz

From the module, the rules, four questions saved as she goes, submit and the result.

Saved as she goes

  • POST /learning/quizzes/{id}/attempts starts or resumes, audited as Created on QuizAttempt
  • PUT /learning/attempts/{id}/answers with every answer and her position
  • One option for multiple choice and true / false; short answers up to 200 characters
  • A shuffled quiz fixes its order for the attempt
  • Locked until its lessons are done: 403 locked

Submit locks the attempt

  • POST /learning/attempts/{id}/submit
  • Scored by QuizScoring on the server
  • Audited as Submitted
  • Then 409 submitted to any change

How it is tested

  • LearningQuizIntroTests, LearningQuizAttemptTests: rules, locks, start, resume, autosave, refusals
  • LearningQuizSubmitTests, LearningQuizResultTests: scoring, locking, limits, feedback, approval, required
  • e2e/tests/learning/quizzes.spec.ts on phone and laptop, offline included, with axe

Things to remember

  • Every answer saves as she goes; an attempt resumes where she stopped
  • Scoring is on the server; a submitted attempt never changes
  • The limit is enforced and her best score counts
