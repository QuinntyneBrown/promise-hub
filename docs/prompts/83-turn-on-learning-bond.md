# Feature 83: Turn on Learning Bond

## Goal

Persona: **Admin / office manager**. Journey "Turn on Learning Bond" in `docs/journeys.md`.

Tom opens `settings-learning-bond`, reads the versioned data-handling agreement (`learning-bond-agreement`: What we collect, Where it goes, What your organization sees), ticks the authority box and accepts it for the organization, or chooses Ask someone else to sign and names a signer (`learning-bond-signer`; the signer's own link is prompt 84). He then chooses coverage (`learning-bond-coverage`: every program or chosen programs, and the status roles, with Admin and Program director fixed) and previews the invitation text in each language the organization's households use, editing only the sender name (`learning-bond-invitation`). A new platform version of the agreement keeps the module on but disables Launch until it is accepted again. Launching itself is prompt 85.

Prompts `01` to `82` in `docs/prompts/` are assumed done; reuse what they built. Frontend libraries: `frontend/projects/learning-bond/`, `frontend/projects/settings/`, `frontend/projects/api/`. Backend feature folders under `backend/src/PromiseHub.*/LearningBond/`.

Why now: nothing reaches a family until an admin has accepted the agreement and said which programs are covered; this is the first visible staff journey of the module.
