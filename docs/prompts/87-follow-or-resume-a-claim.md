# Feature 87: Follow or resume a claim

## Goal

Persona: **Guardian**. Journey "Follow or resume a claim" in `docs/journeys.md`.

After Submit, `learning-bond-submitted` shows "Sent to {provider} on {date}", the fields sent with SINs masked, the estimate, What happens next with `step-marker` and the expected deposit window. The provider reports progress through a signed webhook (`POST /learning-bond/webhooks/providers/{provider}`); each event is applied once by its id, moves the application's status (Received, Deposited, DocumentNeeded) and texts Carmen in her language with the fixed wording. A missing document is uploaded through the link and sent only on Send. A claim that cannot go on (no SIN for the child, taxes not filed, a record fix awaiting staff) pauses on `learning-bond-paused` with plain help and the right government link, offers a reminder, and `LearningBondReminderJob` sends one text after 14 days and one after 30, never to a STOP number. Reopening the link resumes at the paused step with every answer kept. After 180 days with no activity the claim closes as Not completed and its SINs are destroyed.

Prompts `01` to `86` in `docs/prompts/` are assumed done; reuse what they built. Frontend libraries: `frontend/projects/learning-bond/`, `frontend/projects/api/`. Backend feature folders under `backend/src/PromiseHub.*/LearningBond/`.

Why now: it needs a submitted application and a paused claim to exist, which prompt 86 creates.
