# L1 - High-Level Requirements

PromiseHub is a multi-tenant, phone-first system of record for small youth-serving non-profits: households, children, programs, events, and the consent, attendance and reporting that connect them. Sources: the PromiseHub PRD (24 Oct 2026), `AGENTS.md`, and the mocks in `docs/mocks/` (screen ids in parentheses). Phase 1 only; phase 2–4 modules are out of scope. L1-023 to L1-025 bring in Waypoint (ADR 0043); their detailed requirements arrive with the journeys of prompts 50 to 81.

## L1-001: Multi-tenant organizations
Every organization is a tenant whose households, children, staff, settings, modules, plan and audit log belong to it alone. One deployment serves many tenants; no data, search result, message or report ever crosses a tenant boundary. Platform admins manage tenants from one list (`organizations`); a person who belongs to several organizations switches between them explicitly (`switch-organization`). Admins keep their own organization's profile, logo and the defaults of the modules it has on in Settings › Organization (`settings-organization`).

## L1-002: Configurable modules
Every feature is a module (Roster, Permission slips, Roll Call, Quick Cards, Pickup, Messages, Incident log, Reports, Learning, Mentoring, Public website) that an organization switches on or off, from a single module to the whole suite (`settings-modules`, `onboard-modules`). What is off is unreachable on the server and invisible in every client. Turning a module off never deletes data.

## L1-003: Staff identity, roles and access
Staff sign in with email and password, admins with multi-factor authentication, and every action is authorized on the server by role (program director, admin, site lead, youth worker, volunteer coordinator, platform admin, and from Waypoint mentor and volunteer). Youth sign in with a youth account linked to their own child record and reach only their own data; a mentor reaches only the youth assigned to them and a volunteer only their assigned sessions. Child, medical and incident data are visible only to roles that need them. Anyone with an account (staff, youth, mentor or volunteer) recovers a forgotten password through a single-use, expiring emailed link, without PromiseHub revealing whether an address has an account. Admins find every account of their organization in one people directory, change roles and deactivate or reactivate accounts; a youth account follows its child record, and every change is audited. Everyone with an account manages their own personal settings: their profile in the organization they are signed in to, their password, how the app looks to them and which notifications reach them (`account`, `settings-profile`, `change-password`, `settings-display`, `account-notifications`).

## L1-004: Onboarding and time to value
A new organization goes from a confirmed email to a configured organization with its first permission slip sent in under an hour, through short wizards with one question per screen (`onboard-*`, `setup`). A newly invited staff member goes from their invitation to tonight's check-in in two minutes (`accept-invite`, `onboard-staff-*`). A roster imports from a spreadsheet on day one (`import-roster`).

## L1-005: Enroll - households, children and enrollment
One household record holds guardians, children, medical details and approved pickups and is reused for every registration. Guardians enroll or re-confirm through a link without creating an account, and through the same link a young person applies for themselves or a teacher, counsellor or social worker refers one; staff work every application from one pipeline, read who applied and why, keep notes, and place the young person into a cohort, waitlist or decline with a message to the family, and the program roster is then live for slips and attendance. Staff schedule a program's sessions as events, including sessions that repeat every week, and browse them on a calendar; youth on the roster RSVP to them (`families`, `household`, `child`, `programs`, `program`, `roster`, `applications`, `application`, `enroll*`, `events-calendar`, `youth-schedule`).

## L1-006: Slips - permission slips
Staff write one permission slip per event, send it by SMS and email link, and track who has signed. Guardians sign on their phone with a typed name; reminders go out automatically; the site lead sees who is cleared, who is missing a slip and can have a guardian sign on a tablet on the day (`slips`, `slip-editor`, `slip-sign`, `event`, `check-in/day-of`).

## L1-007: Roll Call - check-in, sign-out and attendance
A site lead runs a session from a phone: one tap checks a child in, one tap signs them out, totals update live, and attendance history rolls into program totals and reports with no re-entry. Guardians are alerted when a child is not checked in a configurable number of minutes after start (`check-in`, `attendance-history`, `check-in-sheet`, `end-session`, `correct-attendance`). Mentors take attendance for their own cohorts' sessions with the same roster, and each mark is present, late, excused or absent. Volunteers see the session they are assigned to today and their upcoming schedule, and mark present or absent for their own sessions only (`today/volunteer`, `my-schedule`).

## L1-008: Quick Card - emergency information
Every child has a Quick Card: allergies, medication, conditions, who to call in order, and who may pick up, readable at arm's length on a phone, cached for every child on today's roster so it opens with no signal (`quick-card`).

## L1-009: Pickup - approved adults
Each child has a list of approved pickup adults maintained by the guardian and staff. Sign-out records who collected the child, checked against that list; anyone else requires the guardian's confirmation and the attempt is recorded (`confirm-pickup`, `add-pickup`, `remove-pickup`).

## L1-010: Notify - messages to guardians
Staff send texts, emails and push notifications to guardians filtered by program, age or language, in the household's language, with delivery tracked and bounces flagged. SMS usage is capped per organization and the cap is visible before sending (`messages`, `new-message`, `message`, `guardian-sms`). Staff also publish announcements to a program or the whole organization for youth, mentors, guardians or everyone, sent now or scheduled, which youth and mentors read in the app and guardians receive as messages (`announcements`, `compose-announcement`, `youth-announcements`); Waypoint `L1-ENGAGE-004`.

## L1-011: Incident log
Staff report incidents in a structured form that routes to a supervisor for review. Incident records are restricted to the reporter, supervisors and program directors, and every access is audited (`incidents`, `new-incident`, `incident`, `review-incident`).

## L1-012: Reports and exports
Program directors get grant-ready attendance, demographics and outcome numbers for any period and program, with one-click CSV and funder-template exports; small counts are suppressed to protect children (`reports`, `export-data`). Detailed cohort, youth and staff activity reports and the progress board add learning progress, attendance and mentoring activity while those modules are on, and export exactly what they show (`report-cohort`, `report-youth`, `report-staff`, `progress-board`, `export-report`).

## L1-013: Guardian experience without accounts
Guardians never install an app or create an account. Every guardian page is reached through a link tied to their phone number or email, verified by a six-digit code, in English, Spanish or French, with one action per page (`guardian-home`, `guardian-child`, `guardian-announcements`, `enroll*`, `slip-sign`, `guardian-update`, `survey`).

## L1-014: Mobile first and offline
Every staff flow works in a phone browser. Check-in and Quick Cards work with no connection from the roster cached for today's sessions; actions queue and sync in order when the connection returns, and nothing is lost on a dropped connection (`check-in/offline`, `offline-queue`, `quick-card/offline`).

## L1-015: Privacy, compliance and data rights
Child and medical data are collected for running programs only, never sold or shared. Data residency is selectable per organization; consent records are retained for the period the organization sets; guardians can request export or deletion of their household's data (`settings-privacy`, `guardian-data-request`, `export-data`).

## L1-016: Security
Tenant isolation, encryption in transit and at rest, server-side authorization on every request, protection against the OWASP Top 10, session expiry, MFA for admins and signed, expiring links for guardians (`sign-in`, `session-expired`, `recovery-codes`, `sign-out-all`).

## L1-017: Audit trail
Every change to a child, household, consent, attendance or incident record, every view of medical data and every administrative action is recorded with who, what and when, and is searchable by admins (`audit-log`).

## L1-018: Performance and reliability
Staff pages open in under two seconds on a mid-range phone over 4G, check-in registers in under 300 milliseconds, the service targets 99.5% uptime with daily backups, and background jobs (reminders, alerts, exports) run per tenant and recover from failure.

## L1-019: Accessibility and responsive design
Every screen meets WCAG 2.2 AA (keyboard reachable, labelled, visible focus, sufficient contrast in light and dark themes) and works at every viewport from 360px phones to wide desktops, with a bottom tab bar on phones and a top bar on larger screens, with no horizontal page scroll. Each person chooses a light, dark or device theme, a larger text size and reduced motion, kept with their account (`settings-display`).

## L1-020: Plans, billing and SMS cap
A free plan covers up to 50 active children and a flat Standard plan covers up to 500 for under $100 per month, with no per-guardian or per-volunteer fees. Active-child counts and SMS segments are metered per organization, shown in Billing, and enforced with warnings before limits and a clear path to raise them (`billing`, `change-plan`).

## L1-021: Staff shell - navigation, search and notifications
Staff work inside one shell: a top bar or phone tab bar that shows only the modules that are on, a global search across children, families and events, an in-app notification center that youth, mentors and volunteers have too, updated in near real time and filtered by each person's own notification preferences, and an account menu; the shell never overflows and reflects the active organization (`search`, `notifications`, `bell`, `main-menu`, `account-menu`, `account-notifications`).

## L1-022: Observability and operations
The system records structured logs, request metrics and job outcomes per tenant, raises alerts on error-rate, queue-lag and SMS-cap conditions, migrates the database only through the CLI, seeds idempotent demo data with at least two organizations, and ships a maintenance page for planned downtime (`maintenance`, `server-error`, `offline`).

## L1-023: Learning
Youth work through their program's curriculum on a phone: tracks of modules and lessons unlocked in order, quizzes with attempts and scores, assignments submitted for mentor review, private-by-default reflections, and a journey view of badges and progress. Admins and program directors author the tracks, lessons, quizzes, assignments, badges and progression framework through a draft, review and publish lifecycle. Learning is a module that needs Roster (`learning`, `curriculum`). Traces to Waypoint `L1-LEARN-001` to `L1-LEARN-008` and `L1-CURRIC-001` to `L1-CURRIC-005`.

## L1-024: Mentoring
Mentors see a dashboard and records of only the youth assigned to them, log check-ins, raise and resolve at-risk flags, assess competencies, and, while Learning is on, grade submissions, approve quiz results and assign work. Program directors see the at-risk flags of their programs. While Messages is on, a mentor and each youth he mentors exchange direct messages in near real time under minor safeguards: no message without a guardian's consent for a minor, no channel between an adult and a youth outside the mentoring assignment, and every conversation kept and readable by program directors (`conversations`, `conversation`; Waypoint `L1-ENGAGE-005`, `L1-CONSENT-003`, `L1-NFR-004`). Mentoring is a module that needs Roster (`mentoring`). Traces to Waypoint `L1-MENTOR-001` to `L1-MENTOR-008`.

## L1-025: Public website
Each organization can publish anonymous, read-only public pages about its programs, open programs and ways to apply, volunteer, donate or get in touch, reached by a public website address that names the organization. Only published content is shown, and an organization whose module is off or that is suspended has no public pages. Public website is a module that needs Roster (`website`). Traces to Waypoint `L1-PUB-001`.
