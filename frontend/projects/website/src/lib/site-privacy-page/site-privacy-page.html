What we collect

When you apply, we collect what the enrollment form asks: your child’s name and date of birth, your contact details and anything you tell us about allergies or medication, so we can welcome your child safely.

When you write to us

A message, a volunteer sign-up or a donation keeps your name, email and what you wrote, only so we can reply. We never sell personal information.

Safeguarding

A guardian consents before a child takes part, and every adult who works with children is screened and reference checked. Donations are handled by our payment provider; we never see or store card details.
